LinkedIn Premium Method 🔗

3 months free — the best option

💡

Best option! You get a longer free period (3 months) and you don't need a separate business email.

1

Open LinkedIn Premium Benefits

Go to your LinkedIn Premium benefits page: linkedin.com/premium/my-premium

2

Find the Notion Offer

Look for the Notion offer in your list of Premium perks.

3

Redeem the Offer

Click the offer and follow the steps to redeem it.

4

Enjoy 3 Months Free ✅

You'll get 3 months of free access to Notion's paid AI models — no credit card needed.

1 month free — no LinkedIn needed

1

Go to Notion

Visit the Notion website directly.

2

Sign Up for Business Plan

Select the Business plan during signup.

3

Use a Business Email

You must use a business email address — a personal Gmail or similar may not work.

Important: A business email is needed to qualify for this offer. The good news is that no credit card is required, so there is no risk of being charged.

  • Choose Method 1 if you have LinkedIn Premium — you get more free time.
